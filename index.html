About Me

I am a Professor in the Department of Information Technology at Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology, Kochi. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), and have academic and industry experience spanning more than two decades.

My research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, spatiotemporal data mining, ocean data analytics and visualization, and intelligent methods for environmental applications. My work brings together computing techniques and domain knowledge to study practical problems in areas such as ocean observation and flood susceptibility assessment.
